Act Two

None of that is magic. Here's what makes it real.

For that moment to happen, everything the house knows about a guest has to resolve to one record — then reach the colleague in real time. Every layer below resolves to one golden record.

One · The Foundation

First, she becomes one guest.

Every system that touches a guest — the web, OPERA, the OTA and SynXis channels, enrichment — feeds one place. Informatica MDM resolves them into a single mastered record, so a new booking finds her instead of creating another version of her.

Identity mastered once — match · merge · maintain · purge. Search before create. One record, nothing copied twice.

Two · The Live Moment

Then the house knows what's happening now.

The Golden Profile says who she is. MCP real-time services say what's happening this minute — reservation, room status, arrival signals the instant they change. That's how "her daughter is checking in now" is live, not last night's batch.

Alongside it, Data 360 and Databricks harmonize her history and behavior against that same record — a fast, complete view without copying the data twice.

Three · One Pane

Then it reaches the colleague — wherever they work.

All of it surfaces through Guest IQ — one pane across the front desk, concierge, and mobile. Behind it, one Salesforce platform does the work: Sales Cloud wins & books the stay, Marketing Cloud nurtures between visits, and Agentforce guides the colleague.

The colleague, not the agent, meets the guest at the door already knowing. Every application reads from the record — never around it.

Four · Trust

And all of it is governed end to end.

Consent and privacy are governed in Data 360, secured by Salesforce Shield encryption, with the Einstein Trust Layer keeping agents safe and grounded — and data residency for regional and tenancy control.

For a luxury brand, trust is the product. Every layer resolves to one golden record, under one trust boundary.

Getting There

Prove it on one journey, then expand and scale.

An illustrative path — sequenced to earn confidence before breadth.
Phase 1 · Pilot

Prove the moment

  • Stand up the one-pane experience for a single priority journey — arrival & check-in
  • Read from the Golden Profile for that journey
  • Show recognition at the desk, live
Phase 2 · Expand

Widen the signals

  • Extend real-time MCP signals across more touchpoints
  • Bring Data 360 context & Databricks history into the view
  • Activate segments into Marketing Cloud
Phase 3 · Scale

Everywhere work happens

  • Roll the one pane across concierge, mobile & service teams
  • Ground Agentforce on the unified profile
  • Operate under one trust & privacy boundary
