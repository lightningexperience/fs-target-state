Four Seasons' luxury promise is effortless recognition: every guest known, anticipated, and cared for as an individual across a lifetime of stays. Every interaction should deepen the relationship — personal, seamless, consistent, and trusted.

First, what's at stake
The problem, plainly

Four Seasons needs trusted guest context that reaches employees wherever service happens — front desk, concierge, or mobile — grounded in one Golden Guest Profile, live stay context, and engagement history.

Otherwise, the guest is forced to repeat what Four Seasons should already know.

Four Seasons needs BOTH: one trusted guest profile, and a single pane of glass that brings it to Four Seasons employees.

Act Two

None of that is magic. Here's what makes it real.

For that moment to happen, everything the house knows about a guest has to resolve to one record — then reach the team in real time.

One · The Foundation

First, she becomes one guest.

Informatica MDM unifies guest data from across Four Seasons into a single Golden Guest Profile. New guest information — whether it comes from a booking, a service interaction, a digital channel, or an Agentforce conversation — strengthens that profile instead of creating another version of the guest.

Match finds the same guest. Merge creates one profile. Maintain keeps it true. Search Before Create prevents avoidable duplicates.

Two · The Live Moment

Then the house knows what's happening now.

The Golden Guest Profile says who she is. Real-time API services signal what is happening now — reservation, arrival, room status, and stay context as they change.

Data 360 brings her engagement history together with the Informatica master profile, so Four Seasons employees see both who she is and what is happening right now.

Three · One Pane

Then it reaches the team — wherever they work.

Four Seasons employees should not have to search across systems to understand the guest. The same trusted context should appear wherever service happens — front desk, concierge, mobile, service, or corporate teams.

Salesforce powers the engagement layer: Headless 360 brings the profile into the right experience, Agentforce guides Four Seasons employees, and every app reads from the same Golden Guest Profile instead of creating its own version of the truth.
